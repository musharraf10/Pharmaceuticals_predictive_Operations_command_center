# Pharmaceuticals Predictive Operations Command Center

A state-of-the-art, AI-powered supply chain and operations intelligence platform designed specifically for pharmaceutical manufacturing, procurement, and inventory management.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement & What This Project Solves

Pharmaceutical supply chains face critical operational challenges:
- **Inventory Disruption & Stockouts**: Unexpected shortages of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and critical medications directly impact patient care and lead to costly production downtime.
- **Fragmented Operations & Data Silos**: Procurement, manufacturing batches, quality complaints, and order fulfillment are often tracked across disjointed tools, creating blind spots.
- **Unpredictable Demand Dynamics**: Traditional inventory models fail to capture rapid demand shifts, leading to either stock expiration or unfulfilled hospital/pharmacy orders.
- **Quality & Vendor Reliability Risks**: Inability to continuously monitor supplier lead times, delivery performance, and compliance metrics.

### How This Platform Solves It:
1. **Unified Command Center**: Serves as a single source of truth connecting raw material inventory, active manufacturing batches, supplier networks, and customer order queues in real time.
2. **AI-Driven Predictive Forecasting**: Integrates intelligence models to forecast SKU demand, recommend reorder points, and optimize batch production schedules before stockouts occur.
3. **End-to-End Supplier Management**: Tracks supplier performance, rating metrics, contact personnel, delivery lead times, and active statuses to safeguard procurement reliability.
4. **Automated Audit & Quality Compliance**: Centralizes quality complaints, approval workflows, and system audit logs for full regulatory transparency.
